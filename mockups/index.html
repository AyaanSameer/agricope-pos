Conventions baked into every screen
Money is decimal strings in JSON (never floats) · every query is tenant-scoped by business_id from the JWT · errors follow {"error":{"code","message"}} · sensitive actions (void, refund, big discounts, sent-item removal) go through the manager-PIN modal catching 403 APPROVAL_REQUIRED · cash actions require an open shift.
